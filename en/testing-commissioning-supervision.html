Overview

Testing, Start-up and Supervision

Testing and start-up are the phases where the schedule matters most. An experienced engineer helps detect deviations in time.

YAKA Energies takes part in testing at your suppliers' premises and on site, then assists with the start-up and supervision of your units, so that your schedules are met.

What we do

FAT participation

Factory tests: verification that equipment complies with specifications before shipment.

SAT participation

Site tests: checking that the installed equipment operates correctly.

Start-up assistance

Technical support during energisation and start-up of your units.

Unit supervision

Follow-up of commissioning and of the first period of operation.

  • SAT
  • Commissioning
  • Supervision
