Overview

Renewable energy

Generating part of your own energy on site changes how an installation works electrically: new power flows, protection schemes to be adapted, interface with the grid.

YAKA Energies studies the electrical integration of these solutions in your industrial and commercial projects.

What we do

Renewable energy integration

Electrical integration of renewable generation solutions into the existing installation or the project.

Energy storage

Electrical integration of energy storage solutions.

Grid connection and interfaces

Interfaces with the site's internal network, at LV, MV or HV, and with the grid operator.

Sizing and optimisation

Sizing of the associated electrical installations and well-argued techno-economic choices.
