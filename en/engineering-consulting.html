Overview

Engineering consulting

To optimise the performance of your plant, or before ordering equipment or launching works, you need to know whether the chosen solution is feasible, sized for your needs and economically relevant. That is the role of engineering consulting.

YAKA Energies supports you through every phase of your project (FEED, conceptual design, basic design) as well as on your existing installations, with a single requirement: performance, reliability and cost control.

What we do

Feasibility studies

Verification of the technical and economic feasibility of your project: power supply architecture, site constraints, analysis of scenarios and operation.

Sizing

Sizing of the installations and main equipment, from low-voltage networks to high-voltage substations.

Network analysis

Analysis of your site's electrical network and its interfaces to secure the power supply and performance of your units.

Audit of existing installations

Assessment of your installations, identification of weak points and areas for improvement.

Energy optimisation

Well-argued recommendations to improve the energy performance of your installations and reduce operating costs.

Critical review

Critical review of supplier and engineering office proposals against your needs and specifications.
