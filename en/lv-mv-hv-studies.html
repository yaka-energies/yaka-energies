Overview

LV / MV / HV Studies

Each item of equipment is studied and specified taking into account the whole electrical chain and its environment: LV switchboards, drives, MV switchgear, transformers, motors. Studies, specifications and supplier reviews are carried out by the same expert.

We assist you from detailed engineering through to the testing and commissioning of your equipment.

What we do

LV switchboards and control cabinets

Sizing and specification of main LV switchboards and motor control centres (MCC). Definition of tests. Preparation and validation of diagrams.

Low- and medium-voltage drives

Specification and integration of LV and MV variable speed drives for the efficiency of your units.

Medium voltage (MV)

Specification of switchgear and protection relays, and starting systems. Definition of tests.

High-voltage (HV) substations

Sizing of HV substations and interfaces with the grid operator.

Transformers and HV equipment

Sizing and specification of high-power transformers, synchronous condensers and harmonic filters.

LV and MV motors

Specification of high-power LV and MV motors
(experience up to 60 MW).
