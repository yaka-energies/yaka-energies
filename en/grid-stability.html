Overview

Grid stability & performance

Industrial installations that use power electronics or are connected to weak grids interact with the network. Stability must be verified before commissioning; otherwise problems can remain hidden until an incident occurs.

YAKA Energies analyses the behaviour of your network and proposes well-argued optimisation solutions.

What we do

Grid stability analysis

Study of your electrical network's behaviour under disturbances and load variations.

Stability verification

Verification of the withstand of electrical systems during transient phenomena.

Techno-economic optimisation

Definition of solutions, costing and supporting arguments to improve the performance of your units.

Compensation and filtering

Study of compensation and harmonic filtering solutions suited to your unit (synchronous condenser, STATCOM, filters).
