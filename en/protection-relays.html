Overview

Protection & Selectivity

Poorly set or poorly coordinated protection can cost an entire production run, or even endanger people. Protection and relaying are at the heart of the reliability of your installations.

YAKA Energies reviews your protection schemes, studies selectivity and follows relay testing, from the design office through to commissioning.

What we do

Review of protection schemes

Review of the consistency of the installation's protection schemes, from low voltage to high voltage.

Selectivity studies

Coordination of protection devices to isolate a fault as close as possible to its source, without disrupting the rest of the installation.

Specification of digital relays

Definition of protection functions and digital relays suited to your production.

Follow-up of protection testing

Participation in and follow-up of protection relay testing, at the factory and on site.
