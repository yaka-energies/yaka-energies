About us

An engineering consultancy built on experience.

YAKA Energies brings a constant commitment to performance, reliability and cost control, backed by nearly 25 years of experience in high-power electrical installations.

Founder & Managing Director

Yann AUGUSTIN

For nearly 25 years, I have worked on the design, sizing, commissioning and optimisation of high-power electrical installations for industrial projects. My expertise covers the entire electrical chain, from low-voltage networks to high-voltage substations, combining technical performance, safety and reliability with cost control.

I have designed and specified industrial electrical equipment: main low-voltage switchboards, motor control centres (MCC), medium-voltage switchgear, high-voltage substations, LV and MV variable speed drives, protection relays, power generation units, multi-MVA power transformers and high-power motors of up to 60 MW. This experience has given me in-depth expertise in grid stability and techno-economic optimisation.

My career has been built both within large international groups and in a start-up environment, where I structured engineering organisations and methodologies and supported the skills development of multidisciplinary teams.
