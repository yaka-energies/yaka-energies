Privacy

Privacy & personal data

Last updated: 26 October 2026

YAKA Energies attaches great importance to the privacy of people who contact it. The website yaka-energies.fr is a non-commercial showcase site: it offers no online sales, requires no account creation and uses no audience-measurement tools or advertising cookies.

Data controller

The data controller is YAKA Energies EURL, whose contact details appear in the Legal notice. For any question: contact@yaka-energies.fr.

Contact form and appointment booking

The “Get in touch” button on the Site leads to a contact page available at contact.yaka-energies.fr, provided by our service provider Systeme.io. The information you enter there is sent to YAKA Energies.

Data collected

  • your contact details: name, email address;
  • the content of your message;
  • the information you choose to share about your project via the project description sheet (type of installation, voltage level, power, deadline, description);
  • information related to appointment booking (chosen time slot) and the follow-up of our exchanges.

Please do not send us any sensitive data. Confidential information relating to your installations remains protected by trade secrecy and will be treated as such.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Responding to your request, arranging an appointment and preparing our discussion : pre-contractual measures taken at your request and YAKA Energies' legitimate interest in responding to enquiries addressed to it.
  • Sending you follow-up and reminder messages about your request (appointment reminder, project sheet reminder): your consent, given by ticking the box on the form, which you may withdraw at any time.

Your data is neither sold nor transferred to third parties for commercial purposes.

Recipients and processors

Your data is intended for YAKA Energies. To operate, we use the following service providers, who act as processors:

  • Systeme.io : contact form, contact management and sending of automatic follow-up emails;
  • Google (Google Calendar, Google Meet and, where applicable, Google Forms): appointment booking and video conferencing;
  • Brevo : delivery of emails sent from our mailbox;
  • GitHub : hosting of the Site's pages.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union. In that case, such transfers are governed by the safeguards provided for by the GDPR (European Commission standard contractual clauses or adequacy decision).

Retention period

  • Contact and prospecting data: 3 years from our last exchange, unless you request deletion.
  • If a collaboration begins, the necessary data is kept for the duration of the contractual relationship, then for the applicable statutory retention periods.

Unsubscribing

Each follow-up email contains an unsubscribe link that immediately stops all reminders. You may also write to us at contact@yaka-energies.fr.

Hosting and technical logs

The Site is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages). Like any web host, GitHub may retain technical connection data in its server logs (IP address, date and time of connection, browser type). YAKA Energies has no access to these logs and does not use them. For more information, you may consult the GitHub privacy statement.

Cookies

The Site does not use any audience-measurement or advertising cookies. The contact page hosted by Systeme.io may use cookies strictly necessary for its technical operation; they are not used to track you for commercial purposes.

Your rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act (Loi Informatique et Libertés), you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability of your data, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time and to give instructions regarding the fate of your data after your death.

To exercise these rights, write to us at contact@yaka-energies.fr. We reply within one month. If, after contacting us, you believe your rights are not being respected, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

Changes to this page

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the Site, our tools or regulations. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page.
